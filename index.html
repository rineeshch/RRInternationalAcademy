About Us

RR International Academy

RR International Academy, based in Kochi, is an industry-integrated professional education provider committed to enhancing students’ academic learning through specialized professional programs.

We collaborate with colleges to deliver industry-oriented courses that combine academic education with practical training, professional skill development, and exposure to real-world industry environments.

By connecting academic knowledge with practical learning, we strive to help students understand evolving industry requirements and build a strong foundation for their professional journeys.

Why Us?

Why Choose RR International Academy

Our programs are designed to complement academic education with practical learning, industry awareness, and professional skill development. Through college collaborations, we aim to connect students’ academic knowledge with the requirements of evolving professional environments.

Our approach

We emphasize application-oriented learning, course-specific internship opportunities, technical and communication skills, and exposure to real-world industry environments. Program structure and opportunities vary by course and college.

Mission

B.Com Finance + CIAP

CIAP (Certified Industrial Automation Professional) is a hands-on course that trains you in key automation tools like PLC, SCADA, HMI, and sensors. It’s perfect for those looking to build a strong career in the growing industrial automation field. With CIAP, you’ll gain practical skills and boost your job opportunities in smart industries.

  • Globally Recognized Certification
  • Only 1 Exam
  • Hybrid Internship
  • Personality Development Training

Certified Management Accountant (CMA)

The Certified Management Accountant (CMA) course is a professional certification in management accounting, offering a globally recognized credential for accounting professionals. It's typically pursued by individuals seeking to specialize in the intersection of financial accounting and enterprise finance management.

  • Career Advancement
  • Global Recognition
  • Enhanced Credibility
  • Hybrid Internship
